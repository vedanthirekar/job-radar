# Nabla
- Site: nabla.com · Paris HQ, US office Brooklyn, NY · size 201-500 (Simplify) · 1 tech H-1B filings
- What they do: An ambient AI assistant and dictation tool that writes clinical notes for clinicians, used by 100,000+ clinicians across 190 health systems.
- Hook: Nabla Dictation for Mac (Jul 29, 2026): the full speech pipeline (voice activation, speech-to-text, medical post-processing) runs on-device using Swift, Core ML, and the Apple Neural Engine, integrated with Epic and working with PowerMic and SpeechMike. CPO Laurent Landowski: "Core ML and the Apple Neural Engine made it possible for us to bring our entire speech pipeline on-device for Mac."
- Hook source: https://hlth.com/insights/news/nabla-launches-on-device-clinical-dictation-for-mac-with-epic-integration
- Vedant's angle: Project 990: running open-source models on their own GPU cluster instead of a hosted API; plus HL7 FHIR healthcare ETL project.
